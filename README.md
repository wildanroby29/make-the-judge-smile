One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today — for the [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> My neighbour left a mango on my doorstep with a note that just said 'too many, enjoy.'

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (the grader's `/output` mount). Nothing else runs. The line is the whole entry.
